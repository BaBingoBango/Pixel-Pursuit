# Pixel Pursuit Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 16, 2026_

Pixel Pursuit does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- **No accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.** There is nothing to sign in to and nothing phoning home.
- **No network connections.** The whole game runs on your iPad.
- **Camera.** Pixel Pursuit uses your iPad's camera and ARKit to show its augmented-reality scenes in your room. The camera feed is rendered on screen in real time and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.
- **Puzzle answers.** Anything you type into the game's puzzles stays on the device and is forgotten when you leave the screen.

Questions? Open an issue at [github.com/BaBingoBango/Pixel-Pursuit/issues](https://github.com/BaBingoBango/Pixel-Pursuit/issues).
